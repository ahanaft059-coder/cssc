About Collectorate School Science Club

Discover our heritage, objectives, and the driving force behind youth scientific advancement.

Who We Are

Collectorate School Science Club (CSSC) is an active platform dedicated to nurturing young scientific talents, problem-solvers, and innovators. We cultivate a hands-on learning environment where students explore physics, chemistry, biology, computer programming, and robotics beyond classroom boundaries.

Through annual science fairs, competitive exam coaching, and collaborative project building, our members consistently push the envelope of creative engineering and scientific inquiry.

Our Journey & Establishment

Established in 2026 with a vision to spark logical thinking and curiosity among students, CSSC has rapidly grown into a vibrant academic community within the institution.

From organizing internal lab sessions to competing in national olympiads and science fiestas, our community stands firmly on the pillars of dedication, teamwork, and continuous discovery.

Our Mission

To empower students with deep analytical thinking, practical experimental skills, and technical proficiency in modern science and tech disciplines like coding and robotics.

Our Vision

To build a generation of visionary leaders and creators capable of designing sustainable solutions for future technological and environmental challenges.
